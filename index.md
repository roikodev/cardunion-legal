# Card Union

Card Union is a free app for Battle Spirits players: look up any card, build
decks by the official rules, and share them with other players.

- **Cards:** browse, search and filter every Battle Spirits card, and open any
  card's full text and details.
- **Decks:** build decks that follow the official deck-building rules, save
  them, and share them with a deck code or a QR code.
- **Community:** read and write articles about decks and play, comment, like,
  and browse other players' public decks.
- **Help:** report a problem with the app, or with a card, from inside the app.

Card Union is an unofficial fan app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or
sponsored by Bandai. All card illustrations and card text belong to Bandai.

## Signing in

You can browse cards without an account. To save decks and join the community,
you sign in with your email address, or with Google or Apple. If you choose
Google or Apple, Card Union receives only the name and email address they share
with us, and uses them only to create your account and sign you in. We read
nothing else from your Google or Apple account and share it with no one. The
[Privacy Policy](privacy/) has the details.
